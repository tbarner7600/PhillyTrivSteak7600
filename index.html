Philly Triv-Steak

When was the first Philly cheesesteak made?

The first Philly was made in 1930 by a hungry hot dog cart owner named Pat Olivieri.

Did the Philly cheesesteak originally have cheese?

Believe it or not, the first iteration of the Philly didn't have any cheese on it. Just fried beef scraps and onions atop a roll.

Are there any other iterations of the Philly cheesesteak?

Yes! There are multiple, but the Mushroom Cheesesteak, Pepper Cheesesteak, and the Steak Milano are among the top 3 favored versions.

Where's the "Home" of Philly cheesesteaks now?

The Home of Philly Cheesesteaks is located within South Philadelphia. More specifically in Pat's King of Steaks.
